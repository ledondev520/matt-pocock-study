# Welcome

This is a small reading space for our own study notes inspired by Matt Pocock's public teaching. We are starting with an empty notebook, a little curiosity, and room to learn.

## How to use these notes

- Read in English or 中文 using the language switch above.
- Download any page as Markdown to keep a local copy.
- Visit the original sources to understand each idea in its full context.

These placeholder pages are our own writing. They contain no quotations from Matt's work. Future notes will include links to the specific sources they discuss.

Start with [Matt's public posts](https://x.com/mattpocockuk), explore [his code](https://github.com/mattpocock), or visit [AI Hero](https://www.aihero.dev).
