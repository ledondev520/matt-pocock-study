# Corpus

A continuously updated index of the best of Matt Pocock's public work: one record per post, article or repository, with the source link, date, type, topic tags, engagement numbers and a one-line summary we wrote ourselves. Original texts are not copied.

Search it, filter by type, tag and date, and sort by engagement. The full interface is in progress; data endpoint: `/api/corpus`.
