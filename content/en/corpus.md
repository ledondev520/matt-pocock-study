## Find the source behind a question

Remember a useful idea but cannot place the source? This index helps you locate Matt Pocock's public work, check dates, and read the original context.

Each record contains a link, date, type, topic tags, engagement figures, and summaries written for this site in both languages. A summary helps you find a work; it does not replace it or imply approval of our interpretation.

## How to search

```cards
- **Start with a problem** Search a topic and check whether the summaries address it.
- **Narrow the results** Filter by type, tag, or date range.
- **Choose an order** Use dates to follow changes or engagement to find discussion entry points.
- **Read the original** Check its context, publication date, and conditions.
```

## What belongs in the index

The index covers selected original and quote posts, public X Articles, public article listings, and repository metadata and license information. Replies are excluded by default. All summaries are written for this site.

Records do not contain full posts, comments, X Articles, article bodies, or files from repositories without a license. A link to a work does not establish permission to reproduce it.

```note
kind: inference
Our recommendation: use engagement figures as leads for finding discussions. Publication and collection dates affect the numbers; popularity does not demonstrate that a method works.
```

## Try one search

```exercise
title: Locate a source you can check
- Task: search for a workflow question and open an original link from one record.
- Check: identify its type, date, and a specific claim the source supports.
- No results: change the keyword, remove filters, or widen the date range.
answer: To investigate changing workflow advice, arrange related records by date and read the originals. Keep earlier advice attached to its time and conditions.
```

You are done when you can supply the source link and distinguish supported claims from your own inference. If evidence is missing, leave the question open rather than filling the gap from a summary.
