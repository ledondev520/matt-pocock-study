# About these notes

This is an independent, non-commercial learning project at matt.withkeya.com. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Matt Pocock.

## Our approach

We want to turn our learning into clear questions, small experiments, and thoughtful summaries in English and Chinese. These pages are placeholders for that work, not a complete guide to Matt's ideas.

When we discuss a public lesson, we will link to its original source. Any quotations will be clearly marked and attributed. Please follow [Matt directly](https://x.com/mattpocockuk) to learn from his latest work.
