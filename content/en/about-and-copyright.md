## Who made this site?

This is an unofficial, non-commercial study collection independently written by its maintainers. It is neither Matt Pocock's website nor an official translation, and has no affiliation or endorsement. Public release still requires maintainer review.

## Scope of the sources

The source snapshot ends on 2026-10-20. It covers public posts, public article indexes and open-source repositories with checked licences. We supply our explanations, original examples and a few short quotations. Public access alone grants no copying permission.

Excluded material stays out of pages and downloads. Raw archives and comments are not republished in full; course, cohort and workshop material is excluded. An open-source tool's licence cannot extend to other works it supports.

## Four labels and what they mean

Use our original failed-save scenario to understand the labels. Only the English inside an attributed quote block is the named author's wording. Our explanatory examples are not attributed to Matt.

<!-- compare -->
| Label | How to read it |
| --- | --- |
| Linked short quotation | Check exact English, author, URL and date |
| Site translation | Our Chinese rendering beside a quotation, not another original statement |
| Site inference | For example, our advice to reproduce a failed save before selecting a check |
| MIT adaptation | For example, adapted handoff fields with source file, changes and licence |

## Exercise: classify a statement

```exercise
title: Whose judgement is this?
- Scenario: a maintainer recommends preserving form input after a failed save.
- Task: classify it as Matt's quotation, site inference or MIT adaptation. What evidence is still needed?
answer: It is site advice and should be labelled as inference. Verify that input is retained after failure. Without a source quotation it cannot be attributed to Matt; without adapted source structure it is not an MIT adaptation.
```

Done means you can separate original wording, translation, judgement and adaptation, and identify sources and permissions for actual reuse. Attribution alone grants no permission; non-commercial status supplies no licence.
